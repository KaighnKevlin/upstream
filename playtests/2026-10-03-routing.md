# Blind playtest: Factory → Routing (2026-10-03)

The tester was an agent with no source access. It saw only screenshots, `manual.txt` (the F1 manual text) and the command protocol. Goal: *"Starting fresh in Factory mode, get research to unlock Routing."* SEED=1, harness `tools/scenarios/agent_rec.gd`, run output in `playtests/2026-10-03-routing/` (gitignored), journal at `journal.md` there.

## Result

**Goal not reached.** The run stopped at step 147 of a 150-step cap with Routing at red 0/5. No flask was ever made.

Furthest point (steps 111–127): tapper → shallow mid-air furnace rail → ingots drop into an assembler set to the flask recipe. It could not have worked. A red flask is a **gear + a copper ingot**, and a gear is **an iron ingot + a copper ingot** (`scenes/assembler.gd`). So Routing really costs 5 iron + 10 copper ingots and two recipes, either swapped on one assembler or split across two. The tester's veins were both iron, about 10 pieces each, and nothing in the game ever says what a flask is made of.

Step budget, roughly:

| Steps | Spent on |
|---|---|
| 0–10 | Reading the bar, placing a lab, finding the research screen. This went well. |
| 11–52 | Learning to dig: 42 steps, most of session 1 |
| 53–110 | Getting a tapper to work, then finding a second vein |
| 111–127 | First ingots, assembler recipe |
| 128–147 | Trying to get a flask into a lab, then boxed in |

## Exploits and bugs

- **Building is free in Factory.** Labs, assemblers, tappers and rails all went down with nothing in hand, and RMB removal is instant. If cost is meant to come later, fine. If not, it's the biggest exploit: two labs, as many tappers as there are veins.
- **Rails float in mid-air** with no support. It's useful, and the tester's working chain depended on it. Probably intended for track; flagging it in case it isn't.
- **You can box yourself in** with solid machines and get no hint. RMB removal is the way out, but nothing suggests it.
- **The furnace rail's placement ghost trails a second "next segment"** after placing (`step_068.png`, `step_124.png`). The tester read it as extra rail.
- **Harness (not game):** the step-12 ghost-at-corner effect (cursor handed back between commands), and the status `ore=N` counts loose ore in the world. That's a harness field, not a HUD element.

## Top 5 fixes, ranked

1. **Put the recipe where the player looks: the research screen, the lab label and the assembler.** For example, "red flask = gear + copper ingot; gear = iron + copper ingot", plus text that names the assembler's current recipe and shows the inputs it holds. *Factory Plan:* the core set says "every tier's science can be made from the tiers below it". It can, but the player can't learn how. This one gap made the goal impossible no matter how well the tester played.
2. **A Factory goal line in place of "Ingots in dome 0/20".** A one-line checklist: ore → ingot → gear → flask → lab (Routing 0/5). *Plan:* "Unlock gradually" and "A run is about an hour"; the HUD currently points at the survival-mode goal.
3. **Teach and show digging.** Put "A/D/S + J dig · Space jump" in the hint bar, highlight the target tile, and show a "too hard" puff on ironstone. 42 of 147 steps went here. *Plan:* the miners reaching "ever further along horizontal tunnels" are the start of every run, and right now that's the steepest wall.
4. **Make veins legible:** ore left on the tapper (a gauge or a darkening sprite), a "dry" state, and a starter vein near the Beam that holds both copper and iron with enough for 5 flasks (15 ingots). *Plan:* roadmap item 5, "Ore patches that run dry". Depletion already ships (the plan's table still says "nothing depletes"), but without the feedback the roadmap assumes.
5. **A Factory manual and bar.** Show only the 35 core pieces, filtered to what's unlocked. Add an entry for **the Beam** (lift budget, foot, beam tap). Put all 7 start pieces where they're seen first. *Plan:* "Core set: 35 pieces", "One Beam", and "Height is the currency". The tester never found the Beam loop or the track rail, the two things the plan builds everything on.
